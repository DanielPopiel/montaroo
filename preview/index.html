Why Montaroo exists

Built by an airline pilot who came home with 1,434 clips.

I fly the Boeing 737 for a living. After ten days driving the Grand Circle with my family, I had footage from three phones and my dad’s drone, and no time to sit at a timeline for a week.

Questions

Does it need the internet?

No. Editing, music analysis and place names all work offline. You can switch on more precise place names from OpenStreetMap if you want.

Which cameras work?

Phones (iPhone and Android), DJI drones, GoPro and most cameras that save MP4 or MOV, plus photos. Montaroo mixes them on one timeline by real local time.

How long does it take?

The first draft takes about two minutes, a preview a few more. The final 4K film renders in the background while you do something else.

Can I use my own music?

Yes, any song file on your PC. Make sure you have the rights if you post the film publicly.
