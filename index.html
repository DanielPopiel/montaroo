Before you sign up

The short version

How much does it cost?

The beta is free. After launch Montaroo will be a one-time purchase, no subscription. People on the list get a launch price, and testers who fill in a short survey get 50% off the full version.

When does the beta start?

Early 2027. People on the list get the invitation first.

What computer do I need?

A Windows PC. We’ll share the exact requirements with the beta invitation.

Do you upload my clips anywhere?

No. Montaroo works offline, your clips never leave your computer.
